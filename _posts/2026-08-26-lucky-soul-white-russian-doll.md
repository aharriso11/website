I always enjoyed the rather retro Indie pop of [Lucky Soul's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucky_Soul) first two albums [The Great Unwanted (2007)](https://www.allmusic.com/album/the-great-unwanted-mw0000486640) and [A Coming Of Age (2010)](https://www.allmusic.com/album/a-coming-of-age-mw0001980856), which this lovely track was the first single from. We had quite a wait for their third album Hard Lines (2017) which had quite a different sound to it. They disbanded in 2018.
